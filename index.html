Welcome to MSGED Program

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to the Master of Science in GIS for Environment and Development (MSGED) Program at Jahangirnagar University. As the pioneer professional master's program in Bangladesh integrating geospatial technologies with environmental and developmental studies, we offer a unique interdisciplinary learning experience.

Our curriculum is carefully designed to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical applications. You'll gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge GIS and Remote Sensing tools while developing critical thinking skills to address real-world environmental challenges.

The MSGED program stands out for its strong emphasis on field-based learning, industry collaborations, and research opportunities. Our state-of-the-art geospatial laboratory and experienced faculty will support you in becoming proficient spatial analysts and environmental problem-solvers.

In today's data-driven world, geospatial skills are increasingly valuable across sectors. Our graduates are successfully working in government agencies, research institutions, international organizations, and private companies, contributing to sustainable development nationally and globally.

I encourage you to embrace this opportunity to transform into a skilled professional ready to make meaningful contributions to environmental management and sustainable development.

Professor Ubaydur Rahaman Siddiki

Program Coordinator, MSGED
